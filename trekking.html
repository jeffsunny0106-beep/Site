Machame Route

Often called the “Whiskey” route. It crosses rainforest, Shira Plateau, the Lava Tower area and the Barranco Wall before the summit push from Barafu.

Marangu Route

The only route with dormitory huts. Ascent and descent share the same trail, so scenery is less varied but logistics are simple.
