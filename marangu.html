Marangu Route

The only route with dormitory huts. Ascent and descent share the same trail, so scenery is less varied but logistics are simple.

Route: MaranguDuration: 5–6 daysDifficulty: ModerateScenery: Excellent

Overview

The only route with dormitory huts. Ascent and descent share the same trail, so scenery is less varied but logistics are simple.

Starting point: Marangu Gate, southeast.

Why choose this route?

Best for: Trekkers who prefer sleeping in huts instead of tents.

Daily itinerary

  • Day 1Marangu Gate → Mandara Hut
  • Day 2Mandara → Horombo Hut
  • Day 3Horombo → Kibo Hut
  • Day 4Summit → Horombo
  • Day 5Horombo → Marangu Gate

Itineraries can change with weather and guide decisions.

What to expect

Changing climate zones, long hiking days, cold nights and a very early summit start.
