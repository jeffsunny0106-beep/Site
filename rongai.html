Rongai Route

Starts on the northern side with a gentler gradient and wilder, less-visited scenery before joining the summit approach.

Route: RongaiDuration: 6–7 daysDifficulty: ModerateScenery: Excellent

Overview

Starts on the northern side with a gentler gradient and wilder, less-visited scenery before joining the summit approach.

Starting point: Rongai Gate, north near the Kenyan border.

Why choose this route?

Best for: Trekkers seeking a quieter approach from the north.

Daily itinerary

  • Day 1Rongai Gate → Simba Camp
  • Day 2Simba → Kikelewa
  • Day 3Kikelewa → Mawenzi Tarn
  • Day 4Mawenzi Tarn → Kibo
  • Day 5Summit → Horombo
  • Day 6Horombo → Marangu Gate

Itineraries can change with weather and guide decisions.

What to expect

Changing climate zones, long hiking days, cold nights and a very early summit start.
