Prepare for Kilimanjaro

Get ready

Physical preparation

Build cardio and leg strength with regular hikes and stair training in the months before your trek.

What to pack

  • Hiking boots
  • Warm clothing
  • Waterproof jacket
  • Gloves
  • Headlamp
  • Backpack
  • Water bottle
  • Sleeping bag
  • Personal medication
  • Sun protection

Altitude awareness

Altitude can be challenging for anyone. Follow your guides and safety instructions, and talk to your doctor before travelling.

Safety

Your safety comes first

  • Experienced guides
  • Proper route planning
  • Weather awareness
  • Group monitoring
  • Emergency procedures
  • Appropriate equipment
  • Communication

Mountain trekking carries inherent risks. We work to manage them, and cannot remove them.
