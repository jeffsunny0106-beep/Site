About Kilimanjaro

Location & height

Kilimanjaro is in north-eastern Tanzania, near the town of Moshi. Its highest point, Uhuru Peak, stands at 5,895 m, making it the highest peak in Africa and the highest free-standing mountain in the world.

Three volcanic cones

Kibo (the summit cone), Mawenzi and Shira. Only Kibo is considered dormant rather than extinct.

Climate zones

  • Cultivated farmland
  • Rainforest
  • Heath and moorland
  • Alpine desert
  • Arctic summit zone

Why preparation matters

The altitude, cold and long days make good fitness, the right gear and following your guide essential.
