Northern Circuit

The longest route, circling the quieter northern slopes for panoramic views on a slow, gradual profile.

Route: NorthernDuration: 8–9 daysDifficulty: ChallengingScenery: Excellent

Overview

The longest route, circling the quieter northern slopes for panoramic views on a slow, gradual profile.

Starting point: Lemosho Glades, circling the northern slopes.

Why choose this route?

Best for: Trekkers wanting the longest route and maximum acclimatisation time.

Daily itinerary

  • Day 1Lemosho Glades → Forest Camp
  • Day 2Forest → Shira 1
  • Day 3Shira → Moir Hut
  • Day 4Moir → Buffalo Camp
  • Day 5Buffalo → Third Cave
  • Day 6Third Cave → School Hut
  • Day 7Summit (Uhuru Peak)
  • Day 8Descent → Mweka

Itineraries can change with weather and guide decisions.

What to expect

Changing climate zones, long hiking days, cold nights and a very early summit start.
